How shapes work

A deep specialization starts at L4. One deep domain forms a T, two form a π, and three or more form a comb. Set each domain’s target to design your own profile. An empty level means you have yet to record evidence here.

Case studio

Hardware returns · inventory recovery

In hardware returns, a disposition decision has to be reconciled at serial-number level. The decision is which units can go back into inventory.

Practice scenario · A warehouse logs a receipt and two inspections for the same console. The first inspection clears it for resale. The second flags damage. A refund is already posted. Define the grain and event precedence, identify one ambiguity to escalate, and explain how finance could verify the inventory impact. Record your reasoning in the relevant domain assessment.

Equity research · revenue quality

Use an actual issuer’s annual report from SEC EDGAR. Record two years of revenue, the reporting units and the filing page. Calculate growth and find management’s explanation. In a simulated investment memo, state one alternative explanation and what evidence would distinguish them. Keep reported figures separate from your assumptions.
